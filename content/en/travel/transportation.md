# Accommodation Information for the Host City and Towns of WMG/WMOC 2027 Orienteering

The WMG/WMOC 2027 orienteering competition will be hosted by Yabu City and Kami Town in the Tajima region (northern Hyogo Prefecture), as well as Kamikawa Town in the Naka-harima region.<br>

This area bustles with activity throughout the year: it serves as a ski destination in winter; a venue for mountaineering, hiking, and various outdoor sports from spring through autumn; and a location for "nature schools" where elementary school students experience life amidst the natural environment.<br>
It is a great pleasure to be able to host the World Masters Orienteering Championships in this naturally beautiful region, and we warmly welcome you all.

～～～～～～～～～～～～～～～～～～～～

Here, we have compiled information regarding accommodation and transportation, as these are matters of great interest to you.<br>

While there are relatively few Western-style hotels in this area, we hope you will take the opportunity to experience Japanese-style accommodations—featuring tatami mats and futons—and enjoy our healthy local ingredients and diverse Japanese cuisine.<br>
As shown on the map, the area is quite extensive, and public transportation options are somewhat limited. Therefore, we will be providing official buses from accommodations to the competition venues during the event.<br>

Please note that the accommodations listed here have been confirmed with the host city and towns as being able to accept overseas participants for the orienteering event. There are also other accommodation options in the surrounding area—such as in Toyooka City, Asago City, Tottori City, and Himeji City—all of which are within easy reach if you are traveling by car.<br>

The map shows the following:

   - Official bus routes
   - Major accommodation areas

2. **Accommodation Information**

   - Information provided for each accommodation includes location, access, rooms, meals, foreign language support, access to the event venue, etc.
   - Regarding reservations, in addition to using Online Travel Agencies (OTAs), many facilities accept bookings made directly via their websites or by email.
   - Please contact each facility directly via email or other means to confirm details such as room types, meal options, transportation services, and other details. While many Japanese accommodations include meals in their rates, some also offer "room-only" (no-meal) plans.
   - Most accommodation websites are available only in Japanese; however, you can view them in other languages by using tools such as Google Translate.
There are no shops selling groceries or daily necessities near the forest event area. We have therefore included information on convenience stores and supermarkets in the area.

3. **Official Bus**

   - The organisers plan to operate official buses connecting JR railway stations, accommodation areas, and the event venue.
   - Due to the ongoing difficulty in securing bus drivers in Japan, the official bus service is available only to those who register in advance.
   - Please register via IOF Eventor. Registration deadline:
     - Sunday, February 28, 2027, 23:59 (JST).
   - Official bus fares:
     - Full schedule: ¥15,000
     - Sprint only (3 days): ¥7,000
     - Forest only (5 days): ¥10,000
   - The official bus service originates at JR Yoka Station and JR Kasumi Station, with stops available along the way at roadside stations (*Michi-no-Eki*: Yoka Tajima-no-Kura, Hachikita, and Muraoka Farm Garden). Additionally, the Hachikita accommodation area (Hachikita Tourism Association) and Hachi Kogen (Hachi Kogen Exchange Promotion Centre) are designated as starting points for passengers staying in those areas.Buses will operate between these starting points and the event venue on event days.
   - Please note that the bus routes will differ depending on the day of the event.
   - The operating schedule (timetable) will be announced on the event website after the registration deadline, as it depends on the accommodation locations.
   - You may be assigned a specific bus to use based on your start time on the day of the event.
