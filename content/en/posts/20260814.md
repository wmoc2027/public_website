WMOC 2026 were held in Rzeszow, Poland, from August 8th to 14th.

We promoted next year's Masters Championships to be held in Japan at the Arena. Large tour groups have already made accommodation reservations, and tour operators of some groups will be visiting in the fall for a preliminary inspection. Individual participants asked detailed questions about accommodation and transportation. We also offered an origami experience at the booth to introduce Japanese culture, which was well-received.

The bulletins, flyers, and clear folders printed with maps were gone in no time, making it a very successful promotion.

Since the WMOC has never been held in Asia before, many people seem to be planning a trip to Japan, seeing it as a great opportunity.
