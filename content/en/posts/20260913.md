We have posted a list of nearby accommodations that can accommodate guests during WMOC2027. Please see this page.

[**ACCOMMODATION DIRECTORY**](/travel/accommodation_directory/)

Filter by area and use the search field to find your preferred accommodation using the keywords displayed in the table.

There are three types of accommodations: hotels, traditional Japanese inns (ryokan), and guesthouses (minshuku). Bathrooms and toilets vary; some have private rooms, while others have shared facilities. Please use the keywords provided to narrow down your search for accommodations that meet your criteria.

![](/image/search_accommodation_list.png)


The accommodation name includes a link to the accommodation's website, and the address in the Details column includes a Google Maps link to the accommodation's location.

To make a reservation, please search for the accommodation name on the booking site listed in the OTA and make your reservation.
