* [Bulletin 1 Ver.3](/bulletins/Bulletin1_3.pdf) (25 Jul. 2026) {{< badge title="ENTRY" color="success" >}} Entry for WMOC2027 opens on **July 25 at 10:00 AM** (JST : UTC + 9 hours). For details, please refer "Perticipate" chapter (Page 14) 

  > [!important]
  > As WMOC and WMG are co-hosted, entry synchronization to WMG will be handled through IOF Eventor. Participants in orienteering only must register via Eventor. For those participating in other events, please wait until the event secretariat completes the synchronization process with WMG.
  >
  > If you need to register for other WMG events urgently due to deadlines or capacity constraints, please register with WMG first. In this case, registration for the orienteering event will require an email application.
  > Please entry by email when already WMG have been registered. For further details, please refer to section 16.4 of the bulletin.
