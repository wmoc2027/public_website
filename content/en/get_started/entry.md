## WMOC2027 orienteering competition entry

Entry for WMOC2027 opens on **July 25 at 10:00 AM** (JST : UTC + 9 hours). For details, please refer "Perticipate" chapter (Page 14) of [bulletin 1 rev.3](/bulletins/Bulletin1_3.pdf)

There are two ways to participate in orienteering, depending on whether or not you are participating in other WMG events, as shown in the following diagram.

> [!important]
> If you register through IOF Eventor, you will not be able to participate in other WMG competitions.

### Common requirement

An IOF Eventor account is required. Please refer to the article below and obtain your IOF Eventor ID in advance.

[How to register for IOF Eventor]({{% ref "registration_iof_eventor" %}})

### To those participating only WMOC2027 (Orienteering competition)

Please register at the [IOF Eventor WMOC2027 event page](https://eventor.orienteering.sport/Events/Show/8862)

> [!important]
> Your registration with WMG will be processed as soon as payment is confirmed. Please make your payment promptly upon entry.

### To those also participating in other events at WMG2027

1. Please register to other events at the [WMG2027 entry page](https://entry.wmg2027.jp/provisional-entry)

2. Please register your participation by filling out the required information on the email form linked below. Payment and other procedures will be handled via email.
